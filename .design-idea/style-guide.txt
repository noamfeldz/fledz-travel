Travler AI OS - Design & Concept Style Guide

This guide defines the visual identity, UI/UX principles, and technical styling for the Travler AI OS application. Use these rules to maintain consistency across new features and pages.

1. Core Concept: "The Premium Traveler Terminal"

The design aims to feel like a high-end, AI-powered OS specifically built for travel. It combines the sleekness of dark-mode software with the vibrant energy of global exploration.

Keywords: Cyber-Luxury, Fluidity, Precision, Dark Mode, High-Contrast.

Visual Vibe: Professional but adventurous. Dark backgrounds with "glowing" focal points (Orange accents).

3. Typography

The app uses a mix of two fonts to separate "Content" from "Branding/Headers".

Primary Font (Sans): Assistant (Hebrew & English). Clean, modern, highly readable.

Secondary Font (Headings): Heebo (Weights: 800, 900). Used for large, impactful headers and titles.

Styles:

Headers: font-black tracking-tighter uppercase.

Captions: text-[10px] font-black uppercase tracking-[0.3em] opacity-40.

4. Visual Elements & UI Components

Border Radii: All main containers (Cards, Images, Sections) use large, rounded corners: rounded-[2.5rem] or rounded-3xl.

Borders: Thin, subtle borders to define structure: border border-white/10.

Glassmorphism: Use backdrop-blur-25px for overlays to maintain a sense of depth.

Timeline Logic: A vertical line (border-r-2 border-white/5) connects daily events, with glowing nodes (animate-ping) for active status.

5. Iconography (Lucide-React)

Style: Stroke icons, size={20} or size={24}.

Coloring: Icons are typically zinc-500 (neutral) or orange-500 (active/highlight).

6. Motion & Interaction (Framer Motion)

Interactions should feel "bouncy" and responsive.

Page Transitions: AnimatePresence with opacity: 0 to 1 and x: 20 to 0.

Hover States: - Cards should lift slightly: whileHover={{ y: -10 }}.

Buttons should scale: whileHover={{ scale: 1.05 }}.

Map Paths: Use pathLength animation for SVG lines to simulate "drawing" the travel route.

7. Layout Principles

The 12-Column Grid: On desktop, use a 7:5 split (7 columns for content/timeline, 5 columns for the sticky Map).

Sticky Navigation: - The top navigation is fixed and uses mix-blend-difference to stay visible over any background.

The day selector is sticky at the top of the content area.

Mobile First: Navigation moves to a bottom FixedNav bar on mobile screens.

8. Tone of Voice (Copy)

Languages: Main UI in English, Travel Content in Hebrew (RTL).

Style: Short, punchy, "AI-driven" insights (e.g., "AI Journey Summary").
